# Data Deletion — Zona Segura

**Application:** Zona Segura
**Developer:** Ivan Santiago Torres
**Last updated:** August 13, 2026

[Versión en español](../eliminar-datos/) · **English**

This page explains how to delete the data Zona Segura collects about you, exactly what gets
erased, and how long anything is kept.

## Option 1: Delete it yourself, instantly

This is the fastest route and requires no request and no waiting:

1. Open **Zona Segura**.
2. Tap the **☰** menu icon at the top left of the map screen.
3. Scroll to the bottom of the menu and tap **Leave the group** (shown in red).
4. Confirm by tapping **Leave**.

The moment you confirm, your member record is deleted from our database immediately. If you were
the last person left in the room, the entire room is deleted too, along with all of its safe
zones.

## Option 2: Do nothing

Every room is **deleted automatically 24 hours after it is created**, along with all of its
members' data. This happens on its own, without you asking, whether or not the room is still in
use.

## Option 3: Request it by email

Write to **[ivantr158@gmail.com](mailto:ivantr158@gmail.com)**.

Because we collect no identifying data — no email address, no phone number, no real name — we will
need you to tell us the **room code** and the **nickname** you used so we can locate your record.
We will respond within 30 days at the latest.

Note that if more than 24 hours have passed since the room was created, there will be nothing left
to delete.

## What data is deleted

All of the following is erased under any of the three options:

| Data | When it is deleted |
|---|---|
| Precise location (latitude, longitude and speed) | On leaving the room, or after 24 hours |
| Nickname | On leaving the room, or after 24 hours |
| Status (OK, restroom, food, transport, SOS) | On leaving the room, or after 24 hours |
| Battery level | On leaving the room, or after 24 hours |
| Active route (destination, path, distance and duration) | On leaving the room, or after 24 hours |
| Anonymous device identifier | On leaving the room, or after 24 hours |
| Room data (code, safe zones, administrator) | When the last member leaves, or after 24 hours |

## What data is retained

**None.** We keep no location history, no files, and no records containing personal data after
deletion. There is no additional retention period.

The only caveat is technical and outside our control: our infrastructure provider (Google
Firebase) may hold residual copies in its own systems for a short period as part of its routine
backup processes, until they are overwritten. We have no access to those copies and cannot query
them.

## Data that never left your phone

Your **4-digit PIN**, your language and your colour theme are stored solely in the app's local
storage and are never sent to any server. They are erased when you uninstall the app.
