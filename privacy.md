# Privacy Policy — Zona Segura

**Last updated:** August 13, 2026
**App version:** 2.3.1

[Versión en español](../) · **English** · [How to delete your data](../delete-data/)

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Zona Segura (the "app") is developed and operated by Ivan Santiago Torres, based in Colombia.

For anything related to this policy or to your personal data, write to **[ivantr158@gmail.com](mailto:ivantr158@gmail.com)**.

## 2. Summary in three lines

The app shares your live location with the people in your room so you can look out for each
other. That requires your GPS location, and yes, we do store it while the room is active.
Everything is deleted automatically after 24 hours, and we never ask for your email, your phone
number, or your real name.

## 3. What data we collect

The following data is sent to our servers (Google Firebase) and is visible to the other members
of your room while you are in it:

| Data | Details | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise location** | Latitude, longitude and travel speed | This is the core feature: showing your position to your group and detecting when you leave the safe zone |
| **Background location** | The same location, also while the app is closed or minimised | Zone alarms and SOS would be useless if they stopped working when you lock your phone |
| **Nickname** | The name you type when joining; it can be made up | So your group knows which dot on the map is you |
| **Status** | One of: OK, restroom, food, transport, or SOS | To tell your group what you are doing without typing messages |
| **Battery level** | Your device's charge percentage | So your group knows if you are about to run out of phone |
| **Active route** | Destination, plotted path, distance and estimated duration | So your group can see where you are heading, and to detect detours |
| **Connection status** | Whether you are online, and the time of your last signal | To alert your group if you lose internet or signal |
| **Anonymous identifier** | A random code Firebase generates for your device | To tell one member apart from another within a room |
| **Room data** | Room code, defined safe zones (centre and radius), and who the administrator is | So the group shares the same map and the same alarms |

### Data that stays only on your phone

This data **never** leaves your device and never reaches our servers:

- **Your 4-digit PIN.** It is stored solely in the app's local storage.
- Your language, colour theme, and the record that you accepted the terms.
- Maps downloaded for offline use.

### What we do not collect

We never ask for or store: email address, phone number, real name, ID document, contacts, photos,
microphone, camera, or browsing history. There is no account registration. We do not use
advertising or ad trackers, and we do not build commercial profiles about you.

## 4. How we use your data

We use the data described above **solely** to operate the app: showing positions on your room's
map, triggering alarms when someone leaves a safe zone, transmitting SOS signals, plotting routes,
and showing nearby places.

We do not sell your data. We do not pass it to advertisers, insurers, employers, or data brokers.
We do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

### Legal basis (if you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom)

We process your location on the basis of your **explicit consent** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you
give by accepting the initial notice and granting the system location permission. You can withdraw
that consent at any time by leaving the room or revoking the permission in Android settings. The
app will stop working, but there will be no other consequence.

## 5. Background location

The app requests Android's **background location** permission. We need to be clear about this
because it is the most sensitive permission we ask for:

- It is used **only** to keep sharing your position with your room when the screen is off or the
  app is minimised, and to keep zone alarms and SOS working.
- It is active **only while you belong to a room**. Once you leave the room, the app stops
  collecting your location.
- You can deny it. The app will still work, but your group will stop seeing you once you close the
  screen, and alarms may not fire.
- You can revoke it at any time under Settings → Apps → Zona Segura → Permissions.

## 6. Who your data is shared with

### With the members of your room

Your location, nickname, status, battery and route are visible to **everyone who has your room
code**. Share that code only with people you trust: anyone holding it can join and see where you
are.

### With service providers

| Provider | What it receives | What for |
|---|---|---|
| **Google (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore)** | All the data listed in section 3 | Hosting the real-time database and generating the anonymous identifier |
| **Mapbox** | Your coordinates when you plot a route or search for nearby places, and implicitly the map area you are viewing | Drawing the map, calculating routes, and finding nearby bars, shops, police and hospitals |

These providers process the data on our behalf and under their own privacy policies:
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Mapbox](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy).
We do not authorise either of them to use your data for their own commercial purposes.

Maps include data from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), which receives no
information about you.

### Where the law requires it

We may hand over data if a competent authority compels us to through a valid order. Since data is
deleted after 24 hours, in practice there will almost never be anything to hand over.

## 7. International transfers

Google's and Mapbox's servers are located outside Colombia, primarily in the United States and the
European Union. By using the app, your data is transferred to those countries. Both providers
offer contractual safeguards for these transfers, including the Standard Contractual Clauses
approved by the European Commission.

## 8. How long we keep your data

- **When you leave a room**, your member data is deleted immediately.
- **Every room is deleted automatically 24 hours after it is created**, along with all of its
  members' and zones' data. This is automatic and requires nothing from you.
- A room's administrator can delete it sooner, erasing every member's data.

We keep no location history. There is no archive of where you have been: your previous location is
overwritten by the new one.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its
processing, and request a portable copy. In the European Union and the United Kingdom these rights
come from the GDPR; in Colombia, from Law 1581 of 2012 (Habeas Data); in California, from the
CCPA/CPRA.

In practice, **the fastest way to exercise your right to erasure is to leave the room**: that
deletes your data instantly, with no request and no waiting.

If you prefer a formal request, write to [ivantr158@gmail.com](mailto:ivantr158@gmail.com). We will respond within 30 days at the latest.
Bear in mind that because we collect no identifying data, we may need your room code and nickname
to locate your record, and that if 24 hours have already passed there will be nothing left to
delete.

If you believe we are mishandling your data, you can complain to your country's data protection
authority. In Colombia this is the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio; in the European Union,
the authority of your member state.

## 10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you
are a parent or guardian and believe a child in your care is using the app, remove them from the
room or write to us at [ivantr158@gmail.com](mailto:ivantr158@gmail.com).

If a minor uses the app under family supervision, whoever holds parental responsibility is
responsible for consenting to the processing of their location.

## 11. Security

Communications with our servers are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Access to a room's data is limited
to those who know its code, and rooms are destroyed after 24 hours, which sharply limits how much
information could be exposed in an incident.

Even so, no system is completely secure. Remember that **anyone holding your room code can see
your location**: that is the main route by which your data could reach someone unwanted, and it
depends on who you share the code with.

## 12. Responsible use

Do not use this app to track anyone without their knowledge and consent. Doing so may be a crime
where you live. Zona Segura is built for groups who agree to look after each other, not for one
person to keep watch over another.

The app is also not a substitute for official emergency services. In a real emergency, call your
country's emergency number.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date in the header and publish the new version at
https://skarner1.github.io/zonasegura-legal/. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will notify you inside the app
and ask you to accept it again before continuing.
